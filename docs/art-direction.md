# Art & Audio Direction

The look is the product's biggest differentiator. The city must read as **Kolkata** at a glance.

---

## Visual Target

**Stylized 3D** with **anime-inspired characters** set against **detailed, believable Indian/Bengali architecture**. Think hand-crafted warmth and color, not photorealism and not neon dystopia.

### Pursue
- **Anime-styled characters:** clean cel/half-toon shading, expressive faces, readable silhouettes.
- **Authentic Bengali architecture:** colonial-era facades, ironwork balconies, tram lines, the Howrah Bridge lattice, College Street book stalls, riverside ghats, pandals during Puja.
- **Colorful, lively environments:** saturated but harmonious palettes; streets full of people, signage, hawkers, taxis, trams.
- **Atmosphere & light:** golden monsoon-washed mornings, festival lights, foggy port nights, misty Darjeeling hills.

### Avoid
- ❌ Generic cyberpunk.
- ❌ Neon overload.
- ❌ AI-generated visual clutter / noisy, meaningless detail.
- ❌ Copying Japanese cities directly. New Town's "future" should feel like an *Indian* future.

## Palette & Mood by Region

| Region | Key colors | Mood |
|--------|-----------|------|
| Howrah | Rust, brass, steam-grey, river-brown | Industrious nostalgia |
| College Street | Warm paper-yellow, terracotta, ink-blue | Bohemian, intellectual |
| Park Street | Amber lights, deep night-blue, festive reds | Romantic nightlife |
| New Town | Cool glass-teal, soft white, accent saffron | Restrained near-future |
| Sundarbans | Mangrove green, silt-brown, mist-grey | Tense, natural |
| Darjeeling | Tea-green, cloud-white, sunset-orange | Serene, reflective |
| Digha | Sea-blue, sand-gold, sunset-pink | Light, holiday calm |

## Audio Direction

- **Music:** a fusion score — Rabindra Sangeet & Bengali folk motifs, baul rhythms, and tabla/flute textures blended with modern lo-fi/jazz for daily-life ambience (Park Street gets a jazz lean).
- **Soundscape:** tram bells, Metro announcements, temple/festival drums (dhak during Puja), street vendors, river ferries, monsoon rain.
- **Voice/dialogue:** Bengali-flavored delivery; honorifics and adda cadence even in text.

---

## Character Design Notes
- Modern Bengali fashion grounded in real life: kurtas, sarees and contemporary drapes, college-casual, IT-formal, hill-station layers in Darjeeling.
- Distinct silhouettes per main cast member; expressive "anime" emotive range for social/dating scenes.
- Diversity of age, body type, and background reflecting a real Indian city.
