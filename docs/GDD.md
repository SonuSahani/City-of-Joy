# City of Joy — Game Design Document (GDD)

> Working title: **City of Joy**
> Genre: Anime-styled open-world life-simulation RPG
> Setting: A modern-futuristic West Bengal, India (Kolkata + regional getaways)
> Status: Pre-production / concept (this document is the source of truth for the vision)

---

## 1. Elevator Pitch

You arrive in **Kolkata** — the City of Joy — as a young adult building a life: studying at college, making friends, falling in love, chasing stories, and slowly getting pulled into mysteries that run under the city like the Metro tunnels themselves. Live an ordinary Bengali life by day, and uncover an extraordinary one by night.

It is **Persona 5's** social calendar and bonds, **Yakuza: Like a Dragon's** dense side-activities, and **GTA V's** open-world freedom — but the streets smell of phuchka and roshogolla, the trains are yellow Ambassador taxis and Metro rakes, and the festivals are Durga Puja, not cherry blossoms.

## 2. Vision Statement

Most anime open-world games copy Tokyo, Seoul, or a generic neon future. **City of Joy** is built on a setting almost no game has used: the trams and bookstalls of College Street, the iron lattice of Howrah Bridge, the riverfront ghats of the Hooghly, the mangroves of the Sundarbans, the tea terraces of Darjeeling, and the seaside calm of Digha. The moment a player sees the skyline, it should read unmistakably as **Kolkata** — not "Asian city #7."

## 3. Design Pillars

These are the non-negotiables. Every feature is judged against them.

1. **It must feel like Bengal.** Architecture, food, language, music, transport, weather, festivals, and daily rhythm are specifically Bengali/Indian — never generic. Authenticity is a feature.
2. **A life worth living.** The core loop is *living a life*: relationships, routine, and growth matter as much as plot. The city should be pleasant to simply exist in.
3. **Mystery beneath the everyday.** Ordinary student life is the surface; detective/journalism/crime threads give the world depth and stakes.
4. **Meaningful bonds.** Romanceable and friendship characters are full people with arcs, goals, and flaws — never collectibles. All romanceable characters are adults.
5. **Stylized, not cluttered.** A warm, hand-crafted anime + Indian look. No neon overload, no AI-clutter, no copy-pasted Japanese streets.

## 4. Target Experience / Fantasy

The player should feel:
- The comfort of a **morning cha (tea)** and a tram ride to college.
- The thrill of **piecing together a case** from photos, interviews, and Metro CCTV.
- The warmth of a **Durga Puja pandal-hop** with friends you've grown close to.
- The freedom to **drop everything and take a train to Darjeeling** for the weekend.

## 5. Core Gameplay Loop

```
Daytime  ->  Obligations (college, job, main story beats)
Afternoon -> Free time (build bonds, side activities, explore, investigate)
Evening  ->  Social events, festivals, dates, nightlife
Night    ->  Story/mystery missions, rest, plan tomorrow
            \-> Calendar advances; relationships & skills grow
```

A **calendar + time-of-day system** (à la Persona) frames choice: you can't do everything in a day, so *what you spend time on* defines your character.

## 6. Player Character & Progression

- A customizable young-adult protagonist (new arrival or returning local).
- **Social Stats** (e.g., Courage, Knowledge, Charm, Empathy, Reflexes) gate dialogue, jobs, and bond progression.
- **Skills** unlock through activities: photography, cooking, driving, investigation, etc.
- **Reputation** with factions (college, police, press, neighborhoods) shifts what the city offers you.

## 7. Career / Life Paths (player chooses emphasis)

- **Student Life** — the connective tissue all paths share.
- **Detective Work** — missing persons, corporate scandals, Metro incidents, smuggling.
- **Police Career** — patrol, investigations, traffic, pursuits (optional).
- **Journalism** — photograph, interview, publish, and shape public opinion.

These are not rigid classes; they're overlapping lenses on the same city.

## 8. Tone & Rating Target

- Grounded, warm, character-driven, with mystery/thriller undercurrents.
- Comparable to a 16+ / Teen–Mature life-sim. Crime and investigation themes are present but not gratuitous.
- All romance involves adults and is written as genuine relationship storytelling.

## 9. Scope Philosophy (read before building anything)

The full vision is the scope of several AAA games. We will **not** attempt it all at once. We build in **vertical slices**: small, complete, polished chunks that prove a feel before we widen the world. See [`roadmap.md`](./roadmap.md) for the phased plan and the first playable slice.

> **Rule of thumb:** It is better to have *one block of College Street that feels alive* than ten empty districts.
