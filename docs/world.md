# World Bible — Regions & Map

The world is built as a **central hub city (Kolkata)** plus **three travel regions** reachable by train, ferry, or road. Each region has a distinct mood, palette, music, and activity set so travel always feels meaningful.

> Place names are *inspired by* real locations and reinterpreted for a modern-futuristic, anime-styled fiction. Real geography is a starting point, not a constraint.

---

## Region 1 — Kolkata Metro City (Central Hub)

The heart of the game. Dense, layered, walkable, and connected by a real working transport web. Districts below double as the main social/quest zones.

### Howrah
- The great **railway hub**: cavernous terminus, platforms, departure boards, crowds.
- Bustling **markets** and **industrial zones**.
- **Riverfront** on the Hooghly with ghats and the iconic bridge silhouette.
- *Vibe:* working-class energy, steam-and-steel nostalgia, early-morning porters.

### Salt Lake (Bidhannagar)
- The **IT district**: glass offices, campuses, food courts.
- **Modern apartments**, planned blocks, wide roads.
- **Universities** and **shopping districts**.
- *Vibe:* aspirational, orderly, white-collar daytime / quiet nights.

### New Town
- **Futuristic skyline** and **smart-city infrastructure**.
- **Tech companies**, **high-rise buildings**, sky-bridges, eco-parks.
- *Vibe:* the "tomorrow" of the city — clean lines, near-future tech, restrained sci-fi.

### Park Street
- **Restaurants**, **nightlife**, live music, **events**.
- The glamorous, lit-up evening district; Christmas/New Year spectacle.
- *Vibe:* cosmopolitan, romantic, neon-but-tasteful (see art direction).

### College Street (Boi Para)
- **Universities**, **libraries**, the legendary book-stall maze.
- **Student activities**, coffee houses, adda (long rambling conversations).
- *Vibe:* intellectual, bohemian, the cultural soul of the protagonist's daily life.

### Port Area (inspired by Kolkata Port)
- **Cargo ships**, **warehouses**, container yards.
- **Smuggling investigations** and **crime missions** hub.
- *Vibe:* foggy, edgy, noir — where the mystery threads converge.

### Metro Network (inspired by Kolkata Metro)
- Fully explorable: **stations, trains, maintenance tunnels, platforms**.
- Functions as both **fast travel** and a **mystery setting** (incidents, chases, hidden tunnels).
- *Vibe:* the city's underground nervous system; comforting by day, uncanny by night.

## Region 2 — Digha (Seaside)

- Activities: **beach dates, resort jobs, water sports, festivals, mystery events**.
- *Vibe:* warm, breezy, holiday escape; a place for relationship beats and lighter arcs.

## Region 3 — Sundarbans (Mangrove Delta)

- Activities: **boat travel, wildlife photography, investigations, environmental missions**.
- *Vibe:* lush, tense, atmospheric; nature-driven stories and ecological stakes.

## Region 4 — Darjeeling (Hills)

- Activities: **tea gardens, mountain exploration, railway travel (toy-train), tourism quests**.
- *Vibe:* misty, serene, nostalgic; reflective story moments and scenic exploration.

## Transport Web (how regions connect)

| Mode | Use | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| **Metro** | Fast travel within Kolkata | Also a quest/mystery space |
| **Local trains** | Suburbs & regional links | Crowded, characterful |
| **Ferries** | Hooghly crossings, Sundarbans | Scenic, slower |
| **Buses / Trams** | Surface city travel | Trams are a Kolkata signature |
| **Taxis** | On-demand point-to-point | Yellow Ambassador taxis |
| **Personal vehicles** | Owned cars/bikes | Unlockable, customizable later |

## World-Building Notes (cultural texture)

- **Weather & seasons:** humid summers, the drama of the **monsoon**, crisp winters that frame festival season.
- **Food map:** roadside phuchka, kathi rolls, fish-and-rice thalis, mishti (sweets), the Park Street fine-dining scene, hill-station momos in Darjeeling.
- **Language:** Bengali-flavored dialogue and signage; honorifics (dada/didi), adda culture, code-switching with English/Hindi.
- **Festivals as set-pieces:** Durga Puja (the headline event), Kali Puja/Diwali, Poila Boishakh (Bengali New Year), Saraswati Puja, Christmas on Park Street.
